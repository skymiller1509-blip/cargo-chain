CargoChain Provenance Ledger

Team Butterfly — MetaEarth Hackathon 2026 (HARBOR), Track 1: On-chain finance and trading.

LIVE ON TESTNET — contract deployed 24 Nov 2026, trail verified on-chain

One flow: register a shipment, record a checkpoint at each port or handler, and verify the full trail on-chain. A shipment is verified when its trail has at least 2 checkpoints (origin + at least one more hop).

The demo shipment

RouteLagos, Nigeria → Rotterdam, Netherlands

Verification

CheckpointsVerified?
0No — needs at least 2
1No — needs at least 2
2Yes
3Yes

A checkpoint from a stranger is rejected — only the shipment's registrant or the contract admin may add one.

Run it yourself

git clone <repo-url>
cd cargo-chain
./demo.sh        # runs the full flow, prints the trail above
cargo test       # 10 tests: 8 unit + 2 end-to-end
